# muslim-panel

The day's prayer times under the clock, and beside them three tabs: **Fortification**, the 132 chapters of *Hisnul Muslim* with Arabic, transliteration, translation and a tap counter for each dua; a **Qur'an** verse of the day; and a **hadith** of the day from an-Nawawi's Forty, Hadith Qudsi, Bukhari or Muslim.

- Tap the circle beside a prayer to mark it prayed; the coming prayer is highlighted with a countdown.
- In Fortification, a card suggests the morning, evening, sleep or after-prayer remembrances by the hour. Chapters you've opened work offline.
- Three looks under Appearance in settings (tap the arch): Parchment, Night, and Theme & wallpaper, which uses your Omarchy theme over your wallpaper on frosted glass.
- Settings: automatic location or a city, eight calculation methods, Asr method, Hijri correction, 24-hour clock, transliteration.

It runs no Linux commands. It reads notifications only to show them under the tabs, and installed apps for its dock.

Data: Aladhan (prayer times), hisnmuslim.com, alquran.cloud, fawazahmed0/hadith-api, wttr.in and Open-Meteo. More screenshots and details: https://github.com/thecloudranger/muslim-panel
